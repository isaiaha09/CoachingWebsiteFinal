    About Me

    My name is Isaiah Alvarez. I am a Collegiate || Professional Indy Baseball Player Retiree and a current private baseball instructor in Oxnard, California. I was mainly an Outfielder but also have experience in Pitching, Middle Infield, and Catching. I played baseball all 4 years of high school and graduated from Rio Mesa in 2019. I was on Varsity my Junior and Senior year, with my senior year earning 1st Team All-League. I attended and played baseball at Oxnard College for my first 2 years and got my A.S. for Transfer in Mathematics Honors. From there, I transferred and finished my Collegiate Baseball Career and education at Avila University in Kansas City, Missouri with a B.S. in Computer Science. In the Summer of 2025, I participated in the Pecos League with the Monterey Amberjacks as a Professional Baseball Player.
